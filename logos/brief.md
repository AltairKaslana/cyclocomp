# Design Brief: Zylo
**Essence:** A high-performance cycling data tracker specializing in 'GradeRoad' (slope visualization).
**Audience:** Performance cyclists, data enthusiasts, and athletes.
**Format:** Combination Mark (Icon + Wordmark).
**Style:** Minimal-Geometric, Premium Tech.
**Palette:**
- Background: #0E1013, #1A1D21
- Accent: #FC4C02 (International Orange)
- Text: #FFFFFF, #8A9099
**Constraints:**
- No literal bicycles (avoid generic clipart).
- Must maintain legibility at 16px (favicon).
- Stroke-width >= 6 for outlines.
- ViewBox: 1024x512.
